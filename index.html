About

Who I am, in more than one line

A quick look at where I'm coming from, what I'm building toward, and how I like to work.

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Dar es Salaam (CoICT), and most of my week is spent somewhere between lecture notes, an IDE, and a half-finished side project. I like understanding how things work under the hood whether that's an algorithm, a network, or a user interface and then building something with that understanding.

My interests sit across a few areas: software development (I spend a lot of time in Java and web technologies), computer networking (I think in terms of routers, packets, and topologies almost as much as code), and UI/UX I care about how something looks and feels to use, not just whether it works.

Outside of coursework, I'm usually learning something new a tool, a language, a concept I only half understood in class. I also run a gaming and content brand on the side, which keeps me close to design, audience-building, and shipping things publicly.

Longer term, my goal is to grow from student → developer → professional: to keep building real projects, contribute to teams, and eventually work on technology that people actually use. I try to approach problems methodically understand the problem first, break it down, then build and iterate.
